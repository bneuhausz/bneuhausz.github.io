Lift Recorder

Film your sets without pausing your music.

A tiny Android app for athletes who record their lifts for a coach while their playlist keeps running. It records video only, saves straight to your gallery, lets you trim clips right in the app, and keeps everything on your phone — no account, no uploads.

Privacy Policy • Terms of Use

Your music keeps playing

Spotify, YouTube Music, whatever you train to — it never pauses. The app records video only and never touches the microphone, so Android has no reason to stop your playlist.

Everything stays on your device

Clips are recorded and saved locally. Nothing is uploaded, there is no cloud, and no server ever sees your videos.

Saved straight to your gallery

Every recording lands in your normal device gallery, ready to review or send to your coach with the tools you already use.

Trim clips right in the app

Drag two handles to pick where a clip starts and ends — the preview jumps to that exact frame as you go. Saving keeps the original and writes the trimmed cut to your gallery as a new clip, all on-device with your music still playing.

No account, no sign-up

Open it and record. There is no login, no profile, and no personal data to hand over just to film a set.

Why it exists

On Android, most camera apps request audio focus the moment they open the microphone — and that is what pauses whatever you were listening to. Lift Recorder sidesteps the whole problem by recording video without an audio track. Your coach cares about your movement and form, not gym noise, so the trade is an easy one: the music never stops, and no copyrighted audio ends up baked into a clip you might share.

Free, with an optional upgrade

The app is free and shows a small banner ad served by Google AdMob. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently — once it is active, the ads code is not even loaded. Full details of what is and isn't collected live in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does Lift Recorder pause my music while I record?

No. It records video only and never opens the microphone, so Android keeps Spotify, YouTube Music, or any other app playing while you film your set.

Does it record sound?

No. Recordings are video-only with no audio track. That also means no copyrighted gym music gets baked into a clip you might share with your coach.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Every clip is recorded and stored locally on your phone. There is no cloud and no server ever receives your videos.

Where are my recordings saved?

Straight to your normal device gallery, so you can review or send them to your coach with the tools you already use.

Can I trim my clips in the app?

Yes. Open a clip, drag the two handles to choose the part you want to keep, and save. The original stays untouched and the trimmed version is saved to your gallery as a new clip. Trimming runs on your device — nothing is uploaded — and your music keeps playing while you preview the cut.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. There is no sign-up, login, or profile. Open the app and start recording — there is no personal data to hand over just to film a set.

Is Lift Recorder free?

Yes. The app is free and shows a small AdMob banner ad. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently, and once it is active the ads code is not even loaded.
