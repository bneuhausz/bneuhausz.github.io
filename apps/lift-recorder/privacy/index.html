Lift Recorder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Lift Recorder has no account, no server, and no analytics or crash reporting. Your videos are recorded and stored on your own device and are never uploaded by the app. The only third party involved is Google, through the ads and the in-app purchase described below.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Bálint Neuhausz (Hungary), the data controller under the GDPR. Contact: contact@bneuhausz.dev. Full business details are in the Imprint.

Your videos

  • The app records video only (no audio track) and saves the file to your device's gallery.
  • Videos never leave your device through the app — no upload, no cloud, no sharing done by the app itself.
  • There is no account and no login, so the app does not know who you are.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

In the free version, ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect device and usage information, including your device's Advertising ID, device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction data. Google processes this as an independent controller under its own terms — see Google's Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, the app shows a Google-certified consent prompt before personalized ads are used, so you can accept or decline. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Ad privacy options in the app.

Removing ads

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing — the app never sees your card or payment details. Once ads are removed, the advertising SDK is not loaded at all, so no ad data is collected.

Children

Lift Recorder is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Your rights

There is no account for us to access or delete — uninstalling removes the app's data, and your saved videos stay in your gallery until you delete them. For the ad-related data Google collects, use the Google links above and your device's privacy controls. You can contact us at contact@bneuhausz.dev, and under the GDPR you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the "last updated" date above will be updated.
