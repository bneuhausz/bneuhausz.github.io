Android app

Magyar kártya

Zsírozás, snapszer and makaó — offline, against the machine.

Three Hungarian card games on one hand-drawn Tell-pattern deck, played solo against bots at three difficulty levels. Statistics, 33 achievements, and card sets and tables to collect — all fully offline with no account and nothing uploaded.

Privacy Policy • Terms of Use

Three Hungarian classics

Zsírozás, snapszer (the classic 66) and makaó — three different kinds of game on the same 32-card deck: two trick-takers and one shedding game. Each has its own rules page in the app, so the house rules are never a guess.

Bots that actually play

Three difficulty levels — könnyű, közepes and nehéz. The stronger levels search rather than follow a checklist, and each one was measured over thousands of hands against the level below it before it shipped.

A deck drawn from scratch

The Hungarian (Tell-pattern) deck is original vector artwork made for this app, not a scan — so it is drawn for a phone screen: the corner index stays readable even when you are holding a fan of sixteen cards.

Card sets and tables to collect

Play on the free deck and green felt for as long as you like, or dress the table up: five extra card sets and eight extra tables, five of which are gently animated. They change how the game looks, never how it plays.

Offline, with no account

There is no registration, no login and no cloud. Matches, per-difficulty statistics and 33 achievements live on your phone, and the whole app works in airplane mode.

The three games

Zsírozás

Suits and card order don't matter here: you take a trick by matching the rank that was led — or by dropping a seven, which beats anything. The aces and tens are the zsír — the "fat" the game is named after — 10 points each, 80 in the deck, and a match runs to five game points.

Snapszer

The classic 66, with everything the game is known for: trump exchange, closing the talon, melding twenty and forty, and a strict second phase where you must follow suit and beat the lead.

Makaó

The shedding game: follow the top card's suit or rank and run your hand down to nothing. Sevens chain and stack the draw penalty, an ace skips a turn, a felső goes on anything and calls a suit, an alsó calls a rank — and you must say „makaó" as you play your second-to-last card, or draw four for the silence.

Why it exists

Hungarian card games are easy to learn and hard to find well made on a phone: the apps that exist tend to want an account, a connection, or your patience. This one wants none of them — it deals, the bot thinks, and that is the whole loop, in your pocket and in Hungarian.

The deck was drawn from scratch for the same reason. The Tell pattern Hungarians grew up with is centuries old and long out of copyright, but modern scans of it are not, so every card here is original vector artwork in that tradition — which is also what makes the extra card sets possible, from an embroidered Kalocsai deck to a neon one and a deck set in space.

Free, with optional upgrades

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob — a banner, plus a full-screen ad between hands — with a consent prompt where the GDPR requires one. A one-time in-app purchase removes them permanently. The extra card sets and tables are separate one-time purchases and are purely cosmetic: there is no in-game currency, nothing randomised, and nothing you can buy changes the cards you are dealt. Everything the game does and doesn't collect is spelled out in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Which games can I play?

Three: zsírozás, snapszer (66) and makaó, each against a bot at your choice of three difficulty levels. Where the traditional rules differ from region to region, the app picks one variant and documents it on the game's rules page.

Is Magyar kártya free?

Yes. The game is free and shows ads: a banner, plus a full-screen ad between hands. A one-time in-app purchase removes both permanently. The extra card sets and tables are separate optional purchases that only change the app's looks.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. The games, the bots, your statistics and your achievements all run on your phone — you can play in airplane mode. A connection is only used to serve ads and to send crash reports.

Can I play against other people?

Not today. The app is a solo game against the bots, with no accounts, no lobbies and no online play — that is what lets it work completely offline.

Is there in-game currency or a loot box?

No. There is no in-game currency, no gacha and no randomised purchase. Everything for sale is a named cosmetic you can look at in full before buying, and nothing you can buy affects the cards you are dealt or how the bots play.

What language is the game in?

Hungarian only. The card names, the suits and the game terminology are Hungarian by nature, so the whole interface follows them.

What data does the game collect?

The game itself collects none — no account, no analytics, nothing uploaded. Two things leave your device: crash reports, and the data Google needs to serve ads (with a consent prompt in the EEA). The privacy policy has the full details.

What platforms is it on?

Magyar kártya is an Android game, available for free on Google Play. Other platforms may follow later.
