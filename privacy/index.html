Privacy Policy

This site is my personal blog and it collects very limited data. This page explains what data is collected, how it is used, and how it is handled.

Website Analytics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous traffic patterns. It is privacy-focused: it does not use cookies and does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). I rely on it only to see how the site is used so I can improve it.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, I collect your email address so I can send you updates about new posts and related content. I use MailerLite to manage the subscriber list and send these emails, so your address is stored on MailerLite's servers. You can review MailerLite's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the footer of every email.
